The problem

Your inbox is running your day

Important emails get buried among routine messages, deadlines hide in long threads, and follow-ups slip.

Hours go into sorting, reading and replying, time that should go into clients, strategy and growth. ExecutiveFlow AI does the sorting for you and turns email into a clear plan for the day.

Designed to be safe and dependable

  • No automatic sending: replies stay as drafts until you send them
  • No surprise bookings: calendar events need your approval
  • No duplicates: each email is processed once
  • No invented details: only deadlines and commitments stated in the email are recorded
  • Errors are logged, and you're alerted if something fails
  • Your accounts, your data
Features

What ExecutiveFlow AI does for you

Everything an organised assistant would do with your inbox, running quietly on a schedule.

Email organisation and summarisation

New emails are read on a schedule, summarised in plain English and labelled by priority.

Task extraction

Requests and stated deadlines become tasks with due dates in your task list.

Calendar and meeting coordination

Meeting requests are checked against your Google Calendar and added only after you approve them.

Executive briefings

Each morning you receive a short briefing: important emails, open tasks, deadlines, meetings and follow-ups.

Follow-up reminders

Conversations that need checking back on get a date, so nothing goes quiet by accident.

Email drafting with human approval

When a reply is needed, a draft is saved in the Gmail thread. You edit and send it yourself.

Administrative automation

Routine admin such as logging, filing and status updates runs quietly in the background.

Urgent alerts

Time-sensitive emails are flagged straight away instead of waiting for your next inbox check.

Your own dashboard

A Google Sheets dashboard shows what came in, what's open and what's due, in one place.

FAQ

Questions about ExecutiveFlow AI

Does it work with my Gmail?

Yes. ExecutiveFlow AI works with Gmail and Google Workspace accounts, together with Google Sheets and Google Calendar. Other email providers can be discussed during the consultation.

Will it send emails or book meetings on its own?

No. Replies are saved as drafts for you to review and send, and calendar events are only created after you approve them.

Where is my data kept?

Everything runs in your own Google and Make.com accounts. We use only the access the workflow needs, and you can revoke it at any time.

Is the live demo connected to a real inbox?

No. The demo is a separate workspace that starts empty. You paste in a sample email and see how it's analysed. It connects to no inbox and sends nothing.

Is the price a one-off fee?

Yes. Package prices cover design, setup, testing and handover. Monthly maintenance is optional and quoted separately.

What other costs should I expect?

Make.com charges based on how much the automation runs, and AI features have usage costs. For many small businesses this is a modest monthly amount; we estimate it with you before building. Usage is never unlimited.

Can I start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Many clients start with Starter and add drafting, tasks and calendar features later. Work already done carries over.

Do you work with clients outside Kenya?

Yes. We work remotely and can quote in KES or an equivalent amount in your currency.
